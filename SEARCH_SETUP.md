Launch sequence:

1. Verify the GitHub Pages custom domain and HTTPS.
2. Keep the Cloudflare zone active and preserve the Google/GitHub verification TXT records.
3. Add or verify the Search Console Domain Property `familyboard.win`.
4. Submit the production sitemap once after the final live crawl.
5. Inspect the homepage, one feature, one guide, one tool and one printable.
6. Add Bing Webmaster Tools after Google ownership works.
7. Monitor queries/pages; do not resubmit the sitemap daily or request indexing for all 200 URLs.

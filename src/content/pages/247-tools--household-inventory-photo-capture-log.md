# Household Inventory Photo Capture Log

A home-inventory photo session is useful only when the household knows what area was captured, when privacy was checked, where the protected original lives and when the index needs an update. This free browser tool creates that review trail without uploading images, reading an album or estimating value.

## Choose a small room or item scope

Start with one room, one group of appliances or one move-related area. A short scope makes missing angles visible and avoids turning a shared index into a copy of the household. Keep the original images in the protected storage used by the household.

## Record the capture date and source code

Use a safe photo-session code and the date the image was taken or reviewed. The code should point to a protected folder or backup process, not contain a serial number, address, face or full filename. A source pointer lets the next reviewer reopen the evidence without exposing it in a shared task.

## Complete a privacy check before sharing

Look for people, children, mail, labels, keys, screens, addresses, barcodes and documents in the frame. Crop or mask what the household does not need to share, then record only that the privacy check was observed. Do not paste the image or describe a sensitive detail in the tool.

## Keep an update or backup checkpoint

After a move, purchase, repair, damage event or major room change, review whether the photo index is still representative. A backup confirmation is a separate observation from taking the photo. Follow the current device or storage service instructions; FamilyBoard does not upload, sync, back up or verify the original files.

# Household Meal Preparation and Role Log

Meal planning is easier to hand off when a household can see the meal slot, the source to reopen, the prep role and the next result check. This free browser tool creates that small coordination record without collecting names, addresses, payment details, health information or private messages.

## Plan a meal slot, not an entire lifestyle

Start with one week, a weekend batch-prep session, a travel handoff or the meals affected by a household meeting. Each row should answer what the slot is, which source or pantry reference matters, who prepares the next action and when the household will record what actually happened.

## Keep sources separate from assumptions

A recipe code, a shopping source and a pantry observation are different references. Record them separately. The tool does not create a recipe, calculate nutrition, interpret an allergy, decide a serving size or replace the label and instructions that apply to a particular product.

## Distinguish plan, action and result

“Plan Monday dinner” is a plan. “Household prep role checks the source” is an action. “Meal served and result noted” is a later observation. Keeping those states separate prevents a scheduled meal or a purchased ingredient from being mistaken for a completed household outcome.

## Use roles instead of names

Use labels such as household prep role, shopping role, backup person or travel handoff role. Add a date and a short observation. A shared output then remains useful when family members change, while names, contact details and private schedules stay in the appropriate protected place.

## Future affiliate placement

Meal-planning notebooks, labels or storage products may eventually appear beside this guide, but a product cannot provide nutrition, allergy or food-safety advice. Any commercial block must remain outside the planner controls and never replace the actual label, source or qualified guidance.

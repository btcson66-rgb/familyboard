# Household Repair Evidence Timeline Log

Repair records become confusing when a photograph, a service reply and a family conclusion are treated as the same evidence. A water mark can be photographed without proving its source. A contractor can offer a date without confirming the work is complete. A message that says “fixed” may not describe what was changed or what the household observed afterward. This free browser tool keeps those events in order so the next person can reopen the right source without receiving your private files.

It is not a diagnostic tool, claims adjudicator or photo uploader. Use the [home repair change-order log](/tools/home-repair-change-order-log/) when the question is a scope or price change. Use the [household inventory photo capture log](/tools/household-inventory-photo-capture-log/) when you are planning a privacy-aware photo session. This timeline starts with one observable condition and follows the responsible source's response.

## Set a narrow boundary before collecting evidence

Choose one bathroom ceiling area, one appliance location, one contractor visit, one move-out condition or another bounded event. “The whole house is damaged” is difficult to hand off and easy to overstate. “Water mark on the north bathroom ceiling, visible on 22 August” gives the next reviewer a specific observation without declaring a cause.

The nine-column row is:

`ID | asset or area code | issue scope and temporary boundary | before-work observation date YYYY-MM-DD | evidence pointer and source-checked date | responsible source or response observation | work or follow-up observation date YYYY-MM-DD | owner role | exact status`

Use `BATHROOM-N-A` or `WASHER-B` as a neutral area code. The evidence field can say `PHOTO-A1; source checked 2026-08-23` and point to a protected record. Do not paste the full image, address, contract, quote or private message into the shared row. A source response should distinguish an offered visit, an instruction, an actual action and a later observation.

## Read the timeline as five different events

1. **Before-work observation:** what the household saw, heard or documented on a date.
2. **Evidence pointer:** where an authorized role can reopen the original record.
3. **Responsible-source response:** what the property, manufacturer, provider or contractor actually replied.
4. **Work or change date:** when a stated action or visit occurred, not proof of quality.
5. **Follow-up observation:** what the household could later observe, including an unresolved result.

The tool does not turn one event into another. A before photo is not a cause determination. A scheduled visit is not a completed repair. A completed repair is not a warranty acceptance or proof that the problem will not return. If the source has not answered, leave the row open and name the next responsible role rather than filling the gap with a guess.

## Keep uncertainty visible with statuses

The first statuses move from an issue scope to a before observation, a source response, a work or change record and a follow-up observation. “Timeline reviewed—evidence pointer preserved” means the current history can be reopened; it does not certify the repair, prove liability or decide safety. “Not applicable” requires a reason and a condition that would reopen the event.

When a new visit, scope change or different area occurs, create a new row or version. Do not rewrite a damp mark from last month as though it were today's observation. Preserving dates and source versions lets the household compare what changed without claiming that a third party accepted responsibility.

## Privacy and safety boundary

Use safe codes and protected pointers. Do not enter names, full addresses, phone numbers, account or case numbers, payment details, passwords, full serials, private messages, complete contracts or unredacted photographs. The lightweight pattern check can miss context; inspect every row before sharing.

Entries remain in the current browser profile and are not automatically synchronized, encrypted or backed up by this page. Export an appropriate app backup before clearing browser data. Do not remove covers, cut wires, open plumbing, climb, handle refrigerant or perform a repair to improve the timeline. Electrical, gas, water, structural, mould, height and other safety-sensitive work belongs to current property, manufacturer and qualified-professional instructions.

## Optional affiliate space cannot decide a repair

A future affiliate area might show labels, storage sleeves or general documentation supplies after the educational content. It must disclose the relationship, remain outside the form and be easy to skip. It cannot diagnose the issue, select a material, rank a contractor, promise a repair result or make a purchase necessary to generate this free record.

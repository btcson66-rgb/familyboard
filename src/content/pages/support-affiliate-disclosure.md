# Affiliate Disclosure

Some public FamilyBoard pages may contain clearly labeled affiliate links to household products that directly support the task described on that page. If you follow one of those links and make a qualifying purchase, FamilyBoard may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

## Editorial independence

A commission never determines maintenance advice, safety guidance or which pages are published. FamilyBoard does not call a product “best” without a documented comparison method, and a recommendation block is omitted when it does not help the page's main job.

## Product and price checks

Product availability, specifications and prices can change. Confirm the current listing, compatibility, model number, manufacturer instructions and seller terms before buying. Replacement parts and filters must match the exact equipment model.

Affiliate recommendations never appear inside the private household app, emergency handoff views or print-only sheets.

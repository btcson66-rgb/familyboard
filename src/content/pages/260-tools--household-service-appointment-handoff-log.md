# Household Service Appointment Handoff Log

A technician visit, a cleaning appointment and a quote review each create different household responsibilities. This free tool keeps the service scope, booking source, visit window, preparation, arrival observation and follow-up review in separate fields. It stores no address, phone number or full case file and does not certify provider performance.

## Use a safe source map

Create codes such as `BOOKING-B1`, `QUOTE-Q1` and `REPORT-R1` that point to the controlled booking or document location. A code lets another household role find the current source without copying the address, phone number, names or case narrative into a shareable log.

## Separate preparation from arrival

Before a visit, the household may need to clear an area, locate a model manual or decide who can receive the provider. Those are preparation actions. “Someone arrived,” “the equipment was inspected” and “a report was received” are observations; none of them alone proves that the requested work is complete or acceptable.

## Keep quotes and reports authoritative

Use the provider's written quote, service report and warranty terms for price, scope, coverage and formal next steps. The handoff output only summarizes what the family needs to remember and what remains open. Any future affiliate comparison for tool kits, filters or storage folders belongs outside the workflow with clear disclosure.

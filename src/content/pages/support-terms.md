# Terms of Use

FamilyBoard is a free informational website and local-first household organizer. You remain responsible for reviewing records, backups, reminders and generated checklists before relying on them.

## Local data

The v1 app stores household records in your browser. You are responsible for maintaining current exports and protecting access to your device. Clearing browser storage may remove local records.

## Acceptable use

Do not use the site to harm others, interfere with service, probe systems without authorization or present FamilyBoard outputs as professional certification.

## Availability

The service may change as defects are fixed and the product develops. Roadmap items are plans, not promises of delivery dates.

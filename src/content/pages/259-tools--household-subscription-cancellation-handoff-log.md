# Household Subscription Cancellation Handoff Log

Renewal reminders, trial endings and cancellation confirmations often sit in different provider screens. This free tool separates the service scope, terms or billing source, renewal window, household role, observed cancellation and result review. It stores no account credentials or payment data and does not decide refund rights.

## Use a safe reference

Create a code such as `CANCEL-REVIEW-A` and a general scope such as “streaming service cancellation” or “trial-to-paid review.” Do not enter an email, account number, password, card, full bill or private correspondence.

## Separate a window from an action

A renewal date or trial ending is a source-based window. Opening a settings screen, submitting a cancellation request and seeing a confirmation are household observations. Record them separately so a reminder or a plan is not mistaken for a completed cancellation.

## Keep billing results in the controlling source

The provider's terms, account page and billing record control whether a cancellation took effect or a refund was issued. Use safe source codes in this tool, then return to the controlled source for the formal result. A future affiliate area for planners or folders must remain outside the workflow.

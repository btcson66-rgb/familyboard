# Rental Security Deposit Move-Out Claim Log

Moving out creates several different records at once: the lease, a notice to leave, a condition report, keys or items handed back, a landlord’s deduction explanation, and a refund or account result. Keeping them in one email thread makes it difficult to tell what was promised, what was inspected, and what actually happened. This private worksheet gives each layer a safe reference and a status without asking for names, addresses, account numbers, full lease text, payment details or correspondence.

## Use the log as an evidence index, not a legal claim

Start with the current signed lease and any written move-out instructions. Record a private code for the household and a version code for the lease, deposit record and inspection source. Keep originals in your protected storage. The worksheet can point to `LEASE-L1`, `DEPOSIT-D1` or `INSPECT-I1`; it should not contain the full documents.

Record the inspection arrangement and the actual handoff separately. A scheduled inspection is not proof that an inspection occurred. Likewise, a message saying “we will return the deposit” is not the same as a refund appearing in the account. Use the result field to say what was observed, what source confirmed it, and what remains open.

## Separate ordinary wear from a claimed charge

Do not use a general rule to decide whether a mark, cleaning task or repair is chargeable. The signed lease, local rules, the condition at move-in and move-out, and the landlord’s itemized explanation may all matter. The log has separate space for ordinary wear, cleaning, repair and missing items so you can ask a focused question instead of arguing from a single total.

When a deduction is listed, preserve the date and version of the explanation, the category used by the landlord or manager, and the supporting source they identify. Do not copy private correspondence into this tool. If the explanation is unclear, record that a safe written question was sent and keep the original question and response in protected storage.

## Track the refund or account result independently

A deposit can be described as returned, partially returned, credited, held, deducted or still pending. Those labels are not interchangeable. Record the actual result only after checking the relevant statement, receipt or other source. This tool does not calculate a deposit balance, decide whether a deduction is lawful, or predict when money must be returned. The deadline and delivery instructions in the current lease, formal notice and applicable local rule control.

If the issue remains unresolved, document the next responsible intake rather than entering a legal conclusion. Depending on location and housing type, this might be a landlord or manager response, a local housing or consumer service, mediation, or qualified legal advice. Keep a copy of every submitted request and its delivery evidence outside the worksheet.

## What to keep out of the browser tool

Use only household-safe reference codes. Do not enter a full address, landlord or tenant name, phone number, email address, bank or card information, lease text, notice text, photographs, signatures, access codes, or a detailed private dispute narrative. FamilyBoard’s browser tool is designed for an index and workflow handoff; it is not a document vault, payment service, property inspection, appraisal, accounting system or attorney.

## Affiliate space without interrupting a time-sensitive task

Future general-purpose products such as a document folder, scanner or offline storage card may fit beside this guide, but any recommendation must remain clearly separate from the form. A product cannot prove a condition, create a refund right or replace the current lease and local process. Keep commercial links away from urgent notice, mediation or legal-advice steps.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Does this calculate my security-deposit refund?

No. It organizes sources and statuses. Confirm any amount against the current lease, itemized explanation and actual account result.

#### Is normal wear automatically non-chargeable?

Do not assume that. Compare the signed agreement, move-in and move-out evidence, local rules and the specific explanation provided for this tenancy.

#### Should I paste my landlord’s email into the tool?

No. Store the original privately and enter only a safe source code, date and workflow status.

#### What if I receive a deduction notice after moving out?

Record the notice class, source, delivery and requested response exactly as instructed. Preserve the original and use the notice’s own route and deadline; the worksheet does not calculate one.

#### Can FamilyBoard decide who is right?

No. It cannot inspect a property, authenticate a document, determine liability, provide legal advice or file a complaint. Use the qualified housing, consumer or legal source that applies where the property is located.

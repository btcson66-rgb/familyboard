# Free Home Maintenance Delegation Map

“Someone will handle it” is not a handoff plan. This free browser tool turns a recurring maintenance conversation into a small, reviewable map: the maintenance item, one observable next step, the person accountable for the outcome, the person or service doing the work, a due date and a handoff status. It does not require an account and does not send notifications after the page is closed.

Use a neutral reference such as `MAINT-DELEGATE-2026-A` rather than names or a full address. Source pointers such as `MANUAL-AC1`, `SERVICE-2` or `BUILDING-RULE-1` let the next person find the protected original without copying phone numbers, access codes, account details or private messages into the map. Review the generated summary before sharing it; format checks cannot identify every personal detail.

## Separate outcome ownership from physical work

The outcome owner is the role that notices the deadline, finds the right source, follows up and records what happened. The executor may be another household member, a building manager or a qualified service provider. They can be the same role, but writing both explicitly prevents a handoff from disappearing when plans change.

Write one observable next step per row. “Check the living-room air-conditioner filter against the model manual,” “ask building management who maintains the shared duct,” and “request a qualified service appointment” are reviewable. “Fix the air conditioner” is too broad to hand over. Use a status such as waiting for an external reply when the next action depends on a landlord, building, warranty team or provider.

## Treat dates as review points, not guarantees

Each due date must sit between the current review date and the next review date. That range helps a household revisit stalled work; it is not a promise that a provider will arrive, a part will be available or a fault is safe to ignore. A message sent, payment made or appointment requested is evidence of an action—not proof that the maintenance outcome is complete.

## Use safe source pointers

For gas, electrical, structural, roofing, high-place or disassembly work, assign the safe source-finding step: check the equipment manual, building process, warranty terms or a qualified provider. This map is not a repair diagnosis, safety assessment, permit check or contractor recommendation. Keep full documents and correspondence in the household's protected system and use the responsible official process for decisions.

## Affiliate boundary

Future labels, binders or household organisation products may appear outside the workbench with a clear affiliate disclosure and an easy way to ignore them. They must never be presented as required to divide work, obtain service or make a home safe. The map remains useful without buying anything.

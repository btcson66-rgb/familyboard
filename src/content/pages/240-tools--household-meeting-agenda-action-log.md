# Household Meeting Agenda and Action Log

Short household meetings work best when everyone can see the same agenda, decisions and next checkpoint. This free browser tool creates that structure from safe role labels and topic references. It does not require names, addresses, passwords, payment data, medical details or private correspondence.

## Start with one real meeting outcome

Choose a small purpose: weekly reset, monthly home review, travel handoff, repair decision or follow-up after an incident. Add no more than twelve topics. Each topic should be something the household can discuss, verify or assign—not a full document or a private story.

## Record decisions and open questions separately

Write what the household actually decided, then list what still needs confirmation. A plan such as “review the filter on Saturday” is different from the result “filter reviewed.” Keep an open question open until the responsible person or source confirms it.

## Use roles, not names

Use labels such as household organizer, backup person, partner or handoff recipient. Add a task code and a next checkpoint date. This makes the output useful for a family while avoiding unnecessary personal data in a shared note.

## Keep sensitive material out

Do not paste passwords, door codes, account numbers, card details, health information, children’s private information or full messages. Link to a protected location by a safe reference if the family needs to know where an original is kept. The tool is an agenda and handoff index, not a secure vault.

## Future affiliate placement

General planning notebooks, folders or scanners may eventually appear beside this guide, but a product cannot decide family responsibilities or replace a professional source. Commercial links should never interrupt an urgent, medical, financial or legal discussion.

# Household School Closure Continuity Log

When a typhoon, heavy rain, earthquake, illness notice, facility issue or school event change closes a class or childcare program, the household has to keep several things moving: who is caring for children, who handles transport, which adult changes work, what equipment or routine is needed, and when the next official update is expected. A message in a chat group rarely answers all of those questions.

The free School Closure Continuity Log keeps the notice, household impact, care or transport arrangement, responsible role and recovery review on one local-first timeline. It is not a school information system, attendance record, childcare booking tool or emergency guide. It uses protected source pointers so a family can return to the official announcement without placing child names, student numbers, school logins, addresses or health details in a shared row.

## Capture the official notice and the smallest impact

Choose a neutral code such as `CLOSURE-A` or `REMOTE-B`. Record when the family actually saw the official notice and identify the source with a protected pointer. A school website, municipal announcement, childcare message or public weather authority may control a different question; keep those sources separate when needed.

In the impact field, describe the smallest practical boundary: “class closed for one date; daytime care needs a role” or “activity moved online; device and quiet-space check pending.” Do not add a child's name, student number, account credential, medical detail or exact address. A closure notice is not proof that a particular child is enrolled, attended, safe or eligible for a service.

## Five passes for household continuity

1. **Verify the source.** Point to the official notice and note its publication or checked date. A forwarded screenshot or chat summary can be a lead, but it does not replace the current source.
2. **Map the impact.** Record the affected date, activity or care window and what remains unknown. Do not assume a closure applies to every child, campus or program.
3. **Assign a household role.** Write a role such as household care handoff or household schedule role. Keep names and contact details in an appropriate protected system.
4. **Record the arrangement.** Note a care window, transport plan, work adjustment, meal routine or device check as an observed household arrangement. The tool does not book care, contact a school or guarantee supervision.
5. **Set the next review.** Track the next official update, return-to-school source or end event. Create a new version when the date, program, role or official source changes rather than overwriting the original notice.

## Distinguish plans, handoffs and real confirmation

“A family member can cover the morning” is a proposed arrangement. “The care handoff was confirmed for 8:00” is a different observation. “The school announced a return date” is a source event that still does not certify attendance or transport. Use the status that matches the evidence and leave unanswered questions open. A timeline review means the source pointer, dates and roles were checked; it does not certify care quality, health, safety or education.

The log complements other FamilyBoard pages. A school-record source handoff can track protected education documents. A household activity handoff can cover a routine or event. A family continuity guide can explain broader role design. This page answers the narrower question: what closure source changed the household plan, who owns the next check and when should the family review it?

## Privacy and safety boundaries

Use household codes instead of child names, student numbers, school account credentials, full addresses, phone numbers, health details, access codes or private messages. The tool does not access school systems, send notices, verify enrollment, decide attendance, assess a child's health or safety, book care or transport, or provide education, childcare or emergency advice. Follow the current school, municipal, weather, emergency and qualified-care sources for real decisions. Do not travel or change a care arrangement in hazardous conditions merely to complete a record.

## Optional affiliate space stays out of the workflow

A future commercial panel might show general planners, labels or storage supplies after the educational content. It must be clearly disclosed, optional and easy to skip. It cannot choose a childcare provider, promise supervision, replace an official closure notice or make a purchase necessary to create the free continuity log.

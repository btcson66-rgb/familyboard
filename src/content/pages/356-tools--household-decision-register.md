# Household Decision Register

Household choices become hard to revisit when the question, source, family constraint and final observation are mixed in a chat thread. This free browser tool creates a small, local decision register: one bounded question per row, the source to reopen, the constraints or options considered, the role responsible and what actually happened. It does not vote for a family, rank products, store full documents, access accounts or decide legal, medical, financial or safety outcomes.

## Eight fields keep a decision honest

Each row uses an ID, question, date, source code, constraints or options, observation, role and exact status. “Should we replace the filter?” is a question; “manual source checked” is evidence; “maintenance role scheduled the next check” is an action; “new part observed in use” is a later result. Keeping those stages separate prevents a reminder or agreement from masquerading as a completed outcome.

## Use a neutral code and protected source

Start with `DECISION-REGISTER-2026-A`. Use codes such as `MANUAL-M1`, `BILL-B1` or `MEETING-M2` to point to the protected original. Do not enter names, addresses, account numbers, card data, health notes, passwords, case IDs or full private conversations. The source map should help an authorised role return to the original, not copy it into another shared surface.

## Record constraints, not pressure

Write the household facts that shaped the choice—available time, room coverage, a date window or a role’s capacity—without exposing a person’s private situation. A product advertisement, a neighbour’s preference or an anxious message is a prompt to investigate, not an authoritative conclusion. When a provider, authority or qualified professional controls the answer, leave that source visible and the status open until it responds.

## Review the action and result separately

After a decision, record whether the next action was planned, attempted, observed or still waiting. If the family decides not to proceed, state the reason and the event that would reopen the question. A completed action can still have an unknown result; do not rewrite “request sent” as “service changed” or “item works for everyone.” Version the row when a source or constraint changes.

## Use it in meetings and handoffs

The register can supply one question for a monthly review, a household meeting or a temporary handoff. Ask the next role to repeat the source code, boundary and checkpoint. Link to a maintenance, document, subscription or emergency workflow by code instead of duplicating its records. The tool stores the coordination index locally; it does not grant access to the linked source.

## Privacy check before exporting

Read the generated result once before printing or sharing. Remove identifiers, full locations, payment details, credentials, medical content, private messages and exact account paths from any source you do not need to disclose. The built-in screening catches common patterns but cannot understand every context. Keep the protected originals in the system that controls them.

## Optional recommendations stay outside the form

Notebooks, labels or storage products may eventually appear after the result with a clear affiliate disclosure and a skip option. No product guarantees a good decision or completed action. Commercial content must never obscure the fields, change the status or make purchase a condition of using this free local tool.

## Before closing a row

Can another permitted role state the question and find the current source? Are constraints and options recorded without sensitive details? Are decision, action and result separate? Is the status supported by an observation? Is the reopen condition clear? If not, keep the row open and return to the responsible source.

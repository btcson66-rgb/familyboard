# Household Share Access Review Log

Family documents are easier to hand off when the household can see what was shared, why it was shared, which role needs it and when the scope should end. This free browser tool creates a dated review index with safe codes, without collecting passwords, tokens, private files or account details.

## Define the smallest useful scope

Write a scope such as “document index,” “backup handoff summary” or “travel return checklist.” A scope is not a copy of the document. Keep the original file in its responsible service and share only the minimum index or next action needed for the role.

## Separate role, container and confirmation

The sharing role says who is responsible for the next check. The container code points to the protected place where the share is managed. Confirmation says what was actually observed. An invitation sent, a role assigned or a forwarded message is not proof that access works or that the recipient has read the intended scope.

## Give temporary access an expiry review

Choose an expiry or review date when the share is created. For travel, caregiving or backup handoff, connect that date to the return or role-change event. Do not leave a temporary share open because the original task feels finished; return to the service and record the real revocation result.

## Keep credentials and private content out

Never paste passwords, one-time codes, share URLs, access tokens, account IDs, addresses or full documents into the tool. If a service requires those details, keep them in its protected account or administrator workflow. FamilyBoard only coordinates a safe reference, role and next action.

## Future affiliate placement

Folders, labels, scanners or privacy screens may eventually be recommended beside this guide, but no product can grant, secure or revoke account access. Any commercial block must stay outside the tool controls and never interrupt a user reviewing a sensitive share.

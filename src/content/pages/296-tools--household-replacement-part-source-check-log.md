# Household Replacement-Part Source Check Log

“Replacement filter,” “light bulb” and “battery” sound like simple shopping categories until a household owns two devices that use different sizes, ratings or instructions. A package that looks similar may still be the wrong part. A retailer title may omit a suffix that matters. A family member may remember a brand but not the actual model evidence. The useful record is not a favourite product list; it is a dated link between the real device, the part's purpose, the source that controls fit and the observation that remains unverified.

This free browser tool creates that narrow source-check record. It does not identify a model from a description, search a retailer, compare prices, recommend a brand, validate a part, provide installation instructions or certify equipment safety. Use a neutral reference such as `PART-SOURCE-MAP-2026-A`; do not use a resident's name, address, full serial number or order number.

## Separate ordinary shopping from device-linked parts

Paper towels, detergent and general rubbish bags usually belong in a normal household shopping list. A filter, dust bag, bulb, battery pack, water cartridge or other item that must match a particular device belongs in a source-check log. The distinction is about the evidence needed, not the price. If a wrong choice would require another trip, delay maintenance or create a safety question, it deserves an asset code and a controlling source.

Start with one small scope: the living-room air-conditioner filter, the vacuum dust bag, a printer supply or a lamp's replacement bulb. Do not create a universal catalogue on the first pass. A bounded review makes it possible to tell which rows still need model evidence and which source was actually checked.

## The nine-column row format

Enter each row as:

`ID | asset or part code | part purpose and fit scope | controlling source or model evidence | compatibility observation and basis | quantity and protected storage pointer | owner role | next review date YYYY-MM-DD | exact status`

The asset or part code can be `AC-LIVING-A` or `VACUUM-B`; it is not a substitute for the full model printed on the device. The purpose should explain the household work, such as “filter tied to the actual equipment model,” not simply “filter.” The source field can point to a protected manual, nameplate capture, service document or household record. Keep the full original outside the shared index.

The compatibility observation should say what is known and what is not: “manufacturer list names the applicable range; physical use not yet observed” is more useful than “fits.” If dimensions, voltage, connector, rating, material or regional instruction matters, record that the responsible source must be checked rather than guessing from a photograph or a marketplace title. Quantity and storage are household observations; they do not prove that the item is genuine, suitable or safe.

## Treat five events as different evidence

1. **Source identified:** a manual, label or responsible service source can be found again.
2. **Compatibility basis recorded:** the source states the applicable model or range, or the gap is clearly open.
3. **Item purchased or received:** a household action or package observation occurred.
4. **Item used or installed:** a qualified and appropriate process produced an actual result.
5. **Next review scheduled:** the household has a date to recheck the source, stock or device.

The tool deliberately keeps these apart. “Ordered,” “delivered” or “looks identical” is not a compatibility or installation result. If the package, source and device disagree, leave the row open and identify the manufacturer, seller, service provider or qualified professional who can answer the real question.

## Seven statuses keep uncertainty visible

The first five statuses move the row from creation to source, compatibility, storage and replacement planning. “Review completed—current result preserved” means the household recorded the current observation and its source; it is not a safety certificate or a warranty decision. “Not applicable—reason and reopen event recorded” is for a part that truly no longer belongs in the scope, with a clear event that would bring it back.

When a device is replaced, a manual changes, a part number is superseded, a supplier changes or a safety notice appears, preserve the old row and create a new version. Do not overwrite a previous part code with a new one; the history explains why the household bought, rejected or stored a part at a particular time.

## Privacy and safety boundary

Do not enter full serial numbers, addresses, customer numbers, order numbers, payment details, passwords, private messages or a resident's name. Use neutral codes and a protected source pointer. A lightweight pattern check may catch common identifiers, but it is not a security guarantee. Read every row before printing, downloading or sharing.

The records remain in the current browser profile and are not automatically synchronized, encrypted or backed up by this page. Preserve the appropriate private source and export a suitable app backup before clearing browser data. Do not dismantle, disconnect, test, modify or install a part merely to improve the record. Electrical, gas, refrigerant, pressure, drinking-water, high-access and other safety-sensitive work belongs to the current manufacturer instructions and qualified professionals.

## Optional affiliate space must not choose the part

A future affiliate area could show labelled storage boxes, labels or general replacement-supply categories after the educational content. It must disclose the commercial relationship, remain outside the form and offer a visible skip path. It cannot infer a model, rank substitutes, claim a filter is certified, promise availability or make a purchase necessary to generate the free record.

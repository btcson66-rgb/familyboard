# Free Household Event Duration Calculator

Two timestamps can answer a narrow question: how much calendar time passed between what the household observed first and what it observed later? This free browser calculator handles that subtraction for a power outage, water leak, service visit, delivery handoff or another local record. It does not turn a household observation into an official start, official restoration, service-level measurement, safety result or claim.

## Use a safe label and two real observations

Enter a neutral code such as `OUTAGE-A`, `LEAK-2` or `SERVICE-APRIL`. Do not put a street address, account number, phone number, case number, password or access code in the label. Choose the first timestamp from something someone actually observed or a source you can identify. Choose the second timestamp from a later observation or recorded change.

The date and time fields use a 24-hour clock. Both timestamps must have occurred and the second cannot be earlier than the first. A future appointment is not a completed observation; record it in a planning system instead of presenting it as elapsed event time.

## Read the result as arithmetic, not a verdict

The result reports days, hours and minutes between the two values. A household might write “lights first observed out at 09:00; supply appeared restored at 12:30; local observation gap: 3 hours 30 minutes.” That sentence preserves the observer and the limitation. It should not be rewritten as “the utility outage lasted 3 hours 30 minutes” unless the responsible utility has issued that official duration.

The same boundary applies to leaks, repairs, deliveries and handoffs. “First damp patch observed” to “surface looked dry” is not a drying certificate. “Technician arrived” to “family saw the device running” is not a repair warranty. Keep the result beside the original source pointer and any qualified finding.

## Keep it useful for the next person

Copy the calculation into the relevant FamilyBoard event row with the two source descriptions, the event code and the next review. If the first time is approximate, say so. If the second time comes from a message, building notice or provider record, keep that source separate from household memory. A small arithmetic result is useful only when a later reader can tell which time points it compares.

## Privacy and sharing

The calculator runs in the current browser and does not transmit the fields to FamilyBoard. A downloaded or printed result is still a new copy. Remove addresses, contact details, credentials, medical details, device identifiers and unrelated notes before sharing. Keep complete notices, photos, accounts and contracts in their protected original location.

## Affiliate boundary

Any future commercial panel must be outside the form, clearly disclosed and optional. A label maker, storage product or planner cannot change an elapsed-time result, certify an event or replace the source that controls an official timestamp.

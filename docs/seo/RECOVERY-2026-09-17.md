# FamilyBoard SEO recovery — 2026-09-17

Source data: the Search Console Coverage and Performance exports for
2026-08-18 → 2026-09-14 (last 3 months, web search). All "before" numbers below
were measured against the production build at commit `f2da191`; all "after"
numbers against the build in this change. Nothing here is estimated.

## 1. What the data actually said

Impressions did not decline. They stopped.

| Date | Impressions |
|---|---:|
| 2026-08-24 | 147 (peak) |
| 2026-09-03 | 42 |
| 2026-09-04 | 8 |
| 2026-09-05 | 1 |
| 2026-09-07 → 09-14 | 0, 1, 1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0 |

Over the whole window: 1,053 impressions, 3 clicks, average position 40–50.

Meanwhile Coverage stayed perfectly flat at 115 indexed / 17 not indexed for
24 consecutive days. Pages were still indexed while impressions went to zero, so
this was never a crawling failure — indexed pages that sit at position 40–80 earn
no clicks, and a small further demotion pushes them past the end of the results
entirely. The zero is what position ~80 looks like once the new-site visibility
window closes.

The user confirmed Search Console reports **no manual action**, so this is
algorithmic, not a penalty.

### The number that matters most

The site publishes **1,016 indexable URLs**. Search Console knows about **132**.
**884 pages — 87% of the site — have never been discovered.** No amount of ranking
work matters while seven of every eight pages are invisible.

### The strategic finding

Splitting the Performance export by locale:

| | Pages that surfaced | Impressions | Weighted avg position |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| English | 73 | 1,045 | **42.2** |
| Traditional Chinese | 3 | 43 | **5.8** |

English pages rank on page 5. The three Traditional Chinese pages that surfaced at
all rank on **page 1** — and `/zh-tw/tools/warranty-expiration-calculator/` holds
position 6.09 with 33 impressions, the best volume-and-position combination on the
site. By country: Taiwan CTR 2.56% at position 6.9, against the US at 0.15% and
position 48.6.

Three pages is a small sample and should be treated as a signal, not a proven
rule. But it points the same way as the competitive reality: the English
home-management niche is saturated, and `/zh-tw/` has 515 pages of genuinely
differentiated Taiwan-specific material (長照, 居服員, 家庭防災卡) that almost
nothing else covers. **That is the most under-exploited asset on this domain.**

## 2. Root causes found, and what changed

### 2.1 Sitewide boilerplate drowned every page

`Footer.astro` rendered **265 links on every English page and 433 on every
Traditional Chinese page**. Measured across the 1,025 built pages:

| | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average page HTML | 43,190 B | **17,887 B** |
| Average footer size | 28,846 B | 1,349 B |
| Footer share of page | **67.2%** | **7.5%** |
| Pages >50% footer | 985 / 1,025 | **0** |
| Average links per page | 369 | 44 |
| Total HTML served | 42.2 MB | **17.5 MB** |

Two consequences. Every page was two-thirds identical to every other page, which
is a weak uniqueness signal across a 1,000-page site. And internal link equity was
divided ~370 ways on every page, so nothing was emphasised.

This was safe to cut: `scripts/indexing-audit.mjs` strips `<header|footer|nav|
aside>` before counting inlinks, and it reported **0 orphans both before and
after** — the footer was contributing nothing to the measured link graph. The new
footer carries 18–19 site-level links (collections, product, company, legal) and
keeps every trust and legal page AdSense review requires.

Cutting the HTML in half also halves what Googlebot must download per page, which
matters directly for the 884 undiscovered URLs.

### 2.2 One sitemap, no diagnostics

All 1,016 URLs sat in a single 508 KB `sitemap-0.xml`, and Search Console reports
coverage *per sitemap file*. With one file there was no way to see which part of
the site Google was skipping — the exact question that needed answering.

`scripts/split-sitemap.mjs` now splits the integration's output into ten segments
by locale and section (`sitemap-en-guides.xml`, `sitemap-zh-tw-tools.xml`, …). It
post-processes the generated file, so hreflang alternates, `lastmod` and the
existing filter rules are preserved exactly. `postbuild-audit.mjs` now audits all
segments and still checks hreflang reciprocity across the merged set.

### 2.3 Tool pages served no content to crawlers

`<ToolWorkbench client:only="react" />` meant the tool — the entire point of 113
`/tools/` pages — **did not exist in the HTML at all**. Googlebot saw an empty
div. `/tools/warranty-expiration-calculator/` is the site's best-performing page
(221 impressions, position 16.5) and was competing on its prose alone.

Now server-rendered: the tool heading, instructions, all field labels and inputs
are in the HTML. The submit button ships `disabled` and enables on hydration, so
the pre-hydration window cannot produce a dead click.

The bundle itself is still 1.36 MB raw / 275 KB gzip, because all 226 tool
definitions live in one module with both languages inlined. It is no longer
render-blocking, but splitting it per tool is a separate piece of work and is
queued as such.

### 2.4 317 pages hung off a single contextual link

Excluding the footer, **317 indexable pages had exactly one contextual inlink**.
`src/lib/cluster-links.ts` now links each page to its nearest neighbours on a ring
formed by sorting its cluster by route. Every page gets the same number of inlinks
as outlinks, the links are topical rather than sitewide, and selection is a pure
function of the route list, so builds stay deterministic.

| Contextual inlinks | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Pages with exactly 1 | 317 | **0** |
| Minimum across the site | 1 | **4** |
| Orphans | 0 | 0 |

### 2.5 A metadata bug that was one reordering away from dropping 27 pages

`src/content/pages/` held 54 Traditional Chinese pages that belong to
`src/content/pages-zh-tw/`. Neither page template renders a `/zh-tw/` route from
the English collection, so they produced no HTML — but they leaked sideways:

- **54 duplicate routes** in `src/generated/sitemap-pages.json`, 27 disagreeing
  about `indexable`. `astro.config.mjs` keys these by route into a `Map`, so the
  last entry wins — the sitemap was correct only because the correct entry
  happened to be appended last. A reordering would have silently dropped 27
  indexable zh-TW pages from the sitemap.
- **17 Traditional Chinese links inside the English `/guides/` hub.**
- **37 zh-TW routes in the English search index.**

Fixed at the single point where the English record set is assembled, with a guard
that throws if the master ever defines a `/zh-tw/` route with no counterpart.
Duplicates 54 → 0; cross-locale leakage 17 and 37 → 0. Content similarity also
improved (highest pair 0.267 → 0.170).

### 2.6 The Traditional Chinese tree had no hubs

The English collection hubs have always listed their children — `/guides/` links
to all 343 English guides. The Traditional Chinese templates had no hub logic at
all, so `/zh-tw/guides/` rendered like an ordinary content page with about ten
cards on it. All 515 Traditional Chinese pages therefore had **no top-down
discovery path**: the only routes in were the sitemap, their curated `related`
links, and the cluster ring added above.

That is the locale ranking at position 5.8, so it was the worst possible place for
the crawl to be hardest. The zh-TW templates now mirror the English ones, grouped
by cluster on the guides hub:

| Hub | Card links before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| `/zh-tw/guides/` | 10 | **352** |
| `/zh-tw/tools/` | 10 | **116** |
| `/zh-tw/templates/` | 11 | 17 |
| `/zh-tw/features/` | 11 | 23 |
| `/zh-tw/checklists/` | 10 | 11 |

Minimum contextual inlinks for a Traditional Chinese page is now 6.

### 2.7 Smaller fixes

- **`og:image` was an SVG.** Google, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Slack all ignore
  SVG `og:image`, so every share rendered with no preview. Now a real 1200×630
  PNG, generated dependency-free by the existing icon encoder.
- **No `SearchAction`.** `/search/` existed but was not declared, so no sitelinks
  search box was possible. Added — and `/search/` now actually reads `?q=`, which
  it previously ignored, so the markup is honest.
- **Traditional Chinese pages had no breadcrumb parent level**, giving Google a
  two-level hierarchy where English got three. Both locales now match.

### 2.8 Deliberately not changed

- **Titles.** 443 of 1,025 exceed 60 characters and truncate in results. But every
  one of them front-loads its primary keyword, there are **zero duplicate titles
  and zero duplicate descriptions** across 1,025 pages, and at position 40–80
  truncation is not what is suppressing clicks. Worth a pass once rankings move;
  not a cause of the collapse.
- **FAQ markup.** 981 pages carry `FAQPage`. Google restricted FAQ rich results to
  authoritative government and health sites in August 2023, so this earns nothing
  here — consistent with the Search Appearance export being completely empty. It
  is harmless and still useful to answer-extraction systems, so it stays, but it
  is not a rich-result strategy.
- **Page count.** No pages were added or removed, per `docs/seo/SEO_RULES.md`.

## 3. What to do next, in order

1. **Deploy, then resubmit the sitemap index once.** Watch the per-segment
   coverage numbers — that is now the instrument for finding where discovery
   stalls.
2. **Run `npm run indexnow:submit`** after the deploy. The IndexNow key is already
   live at `/06049c9105ef4f959d905baefcd0b0be.txt` and the script walks the
   sitemap index, so it picks up all ten segments unchanged. This pushes Bing and
   Yandex immediately; Google does not use IndexNow, but Bing indexing is real
   traffic the site is currently getting none of, and it costs one command.
3. **Enable the Cloudflare proxy** and apply `docs/seo/CLOUDFLARE.md`: real 301s
   for the two merged routes, Brotli, and immutable caching for `/_astro/`.
4. **Push the Traditional Chinese side.** It is 515 pages ranking on page 1 where
   they surface, in a market with a fraction of the English competition, and it is
   the clearest path to volume on this domain.
5. **Get the first real backlinks.** Nothing in this change creates authority, and
   for a domain this young authority is the binding constraint on both indexing
   rate and position.
6. **Split the tool bundle** so a tool page loads only its own definition.

## 4. On the 10,000 impressions/day target

The arithmetic works: roughly 1,000 indexed pages averaging 10 impressions a day
each reaches it. Both halves of that are currently missing — 115 pages are
indexed, not 1,000, and the site is at 0 impressions, not 10 per page.

This change removes the technical reasons Google had to ignore the other 884
pages, and removes the reasons those pages looked like near-duplicates of each
other. It does not create authority, and it cannot make Google index faster than
it chooses to. Expect discovery to move over weeks, not days, and expect the
Traditional Chinese section to move first, because that is where this domain
already ranks.
